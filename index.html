About Me

From Raw Data to Trusted Platforms

I'm a Data Engineer at Verizon, where I build scheduled GCP pipelines into BigQuery, automate revenue-assurance reconciliation, deliver Looker dashboards on a LookML semantic layer, and validated a 125-process Teradata-to-BigQuery migration before cutover.

I got here by working every layer of the data stack. At Accenture I learned enterprise foundations: ETL/ELT across OLTP and OLAP systems and SQL tuning across 30+ source systems. At UNT I engineered ingestion and star-schema models over 3M+ healthcare records. At Wisdom Index Advisors I moved a firm from spreadsheets to a cloud database, an analytics app, and a GPT-4 review agent. Holiday Channel was medallion pipelines in PySpark, and Cates Control Systems was turning raw ERP tables into a model the business could trust.

What ties it together: I define grain and keys before I build, weigh platform trade-offs up front, and reconcile every load, so the numbers hold from source to report.

  • Cloud data platforms: Fabric, BigQuery/GCP, Azure, Databricks, Snowflake
  • Dimensional modeling: grain, facts, dimensions, keys, star schemas
  • Migration validation & reconciliation (Teradata → BigQuery)
  • Semantic layers in Power BI & Looker/LookML with row-level security
  • GenAI in the pipeline: GPT-4, RAG, LangChain & LangGraph
